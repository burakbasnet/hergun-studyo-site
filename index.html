HerGün Stüdyo

HerGün Stüdyo is the private production studio of the language-learning pages @hergunalmanca_ (German for Turkish speakers) and @herguningilizce_0 (English for Turkish speakers).

It prepares short A1-level lesson and quiz videos and publishes them to the owner's own social media accounts (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) at scheduled times. It is not a public service.

This app uses YouTube API Services to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel.
